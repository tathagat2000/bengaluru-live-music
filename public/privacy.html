Privacy

Bengaluru Live uses Google Sign-In only to verify your identity. We store your Google subject identifier, verified email address, and your wishlist or “not interested” choices so they can persist across devices.

The concert catalogue is read from a private source spreadsheet by the application backend. Your browser does not receive spreadsheet credentials, and your preference choices are not shown to other users.

To request deletion of your account preferences, open an issue in the project repository. We do not sell personal information.
